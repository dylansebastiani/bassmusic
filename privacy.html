Privacy

This page explains what collects when you join , why, and how to get it deleted.

What we collect

Your email address when you join. Optionally, if you give them: first name, ZIP code and phone number. We also record which link brought you here (Instagram, TikTok, a show QR code, a friend's referral link), a hashed version of your IP address for abuse prevention (never the raw address), which tracks you unlock and play, and which emails you open.

Why

To send you unreleased music, show announcements, presales and member-only drops; to see which shows people scan at; to work out which cities to book shows in; and to stop bots and duplicate signups.

Texts

We only text you if you gave a phone number and ticked the consent box. We store the exact wording you agreed to and when. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.

On your phone

After you join, your browser keeps a private member key so you don't have to sign in again and show codes you scan land on your account. It never contains your email. Tap "Sign out" on your collection page, or clear this site's data, to remove it.

Who we share it with

Our email provider (to deliver email) and our hosting and database providers (to run this site). We may share hashed versions of member emails and phone numbers with Meta and TikTok so we can show ads to members and to people like them. Hashing means they receive a scrambled version they can only match against accounts they already hold. We never sell your information.

How to opt out or delete

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. To be removed from ad audiences or to have everything we hold about you deleted, email from the address you signed up with and we'll do it within 30 days.

Age

You must be 13 or older to join.
