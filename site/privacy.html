Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026.

This page explains what collects when you buy a ticket, join the member list, sell tickets or join the artist waitlist, why, and how to get it deleted.

What we collect

When you buy a ticket: your name and email address, the tickets you bought, what you paid, any promo code you used, and when your ticket is scanned at the door. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number goes straight to Stripe and never touches our servers.

When you join the member list: your email address. Optionally, if you give them: first name, ZIP code and phone number. Ticket buyers are added to the member list too, and can unsubscribe at any time.

When you sell tickets (promoters): your email address, your crew or company name, the show details and art you upload, and the payout account you connect through Stripe. Stripe collects and holds your banking and identity details. We never see them.

When you join the artist waitlist or try a test ticket: the details you type in, such as artist name, full name, crew name, email address and phone number.

On every visit: which link brought you here (Instagram, TikTok, a show QR code, a friend's referral link), a hashed version of your IP address for abuse prevention (never the raw address), which tracks you unlock and play, and which emails you open.

Why

To issue and email your tickets and check you in at the door; to pay promoters for their sales; to send you unreleased music, show announcements, presales and member-only drops; to tell artists when the artist portal opens; to see which shows people scan at; to work out which cities to book shows in; and to stop bots, fraud and duplicate signups.

Texts

We only text you if you gave a phone number and ticked the consent box. We store the exact wording you agreed to and when. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.

On your phone

After you join, your browser keeps a private member key so you don't have to sign in again and show codes you scan land on your account. It never contains your email. Tap "Sign out" on your collection page, or clear this site's data, to remove it.

Who we share it with

The promoter of your show. When you buy a ticket, the promoter running that show receives your name, email address and order so they can run the door, contact you about the show and keep their own list of buyers. Once they have it, their own privacy practices apply to their copy.

Stripe, to take payments and pay promoters. Our email provider, to deliver email. Our hosting and database providers, to run this site.

Ad platforms. Meta, Google and TikTok receive page and funnel events as described in the cookies, pixels and analytics section below. We do not send them names, emails, phone numbers or payment details.

We never sell your information.

Cookies, pixels and analytics

We use Meta Pixel, Google Analytics and TikTok Pixel on our public pages. They help us see how many people visit, which pages they look at, and how they move through buying a ticket. We use this to improve the site and to measure our ads on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

What they collect. Pages you visit, ticket steps you reach (like viewing a show, starting checkout and finishing a purchase), your browser and device type, your general location, and your IP address. Meta, Google and TikTok may set or read cookies in your browser to do this.

What we do not send them. We do not send your name, email, phone number or payment details to Meta, Google or TikTok. These tools do not run on account, login or door check-in pages.

What Meta, Google and TikTok do with it. They receive this data under their own privacy policies. Depending on your settings with them, they may use it to show you ads, including Subduction ads.

How to opt out.

  • Google Analytics: install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google ads: change your settings in My Ad Center. Google explains how it uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Meta: change your settings in Facebook ad preferences. Meta explains its cookies in its Cookies Policy.
  • TikTok: change your settings in TikTok's Privacy Policy and in the ad settings of your TikTok account. Opt-out options are described in its Cookies Policy.
  • Many ad companies: use the WebChoices tool.
  • Any site: block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

Some events have their own page with separate tracking run under that event's own settings. This section covers Subduction's own public pages.

How long we keep it

Ticket and payment records are kept as long as we need them for refunds, disputes, tax and accounting. Everything else is kept until you ask us to delete it or unsubscribe and ask for removal.

How to opt out or delete

Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe. To be removed from ad audiences or to have everything we hold about you deleted, email from the address you signed up with and we'll do it within 30 days. Records we're required to keep for payments and tax are kept only as long as the law requires.

Age

You must be 13 or older to join the member list. Shows set their own age limits, shown on each show's page.
