Giveaway rules

No purchase necessary. Joining is free and is the only way to enter. Buying a ticket or anything else does not improve your chances.

Who can enter

Members 18 or older, or 21 or older where the venue is 21+. Employees of and performing artists for that event aren't eligible. Void where prohibited.

How entries work

Scanning an event's QR code and joining (or being a member and scanning it) gives you one entry for that event. Referral rewards may add extra entries, as listed on your member page. One account per person.

How winners are picked

Randomly, from all entries for that event, weighted by entries. The winner is announced at the event or by email. If a winner can't be reached or doesn't show, we may draw again.

Prizes

The prize for each event is shown on the signup page for that event. No cash alternative. Prizes can't be transferred.
