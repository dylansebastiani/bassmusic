Estimates, not quotes. Read in November 2026 from each platform's public page. A negotiated rate can be different, and fees change. Every number here is for one ticket per order, with the buyer paying the fee and no tax added.

Subduction matches checkout. Buyers pay a flat $1.50 per paid ticket plus Stripe card processing of 2.9% and 30¢ per order. The total is rounded up so you keep the full ticket price. Free tickets stay free.

Eventbrite's published US rate is 3.7% + $1.79 per paid ticket, plus 2.9% payment processing on the order, including that service fee. Eventbrite pricing.

Posh's published rate is 10% + $0.99 per paid ticket, and card processing is included. Posh fees.

DICE's US self sign-up terms charge 13% of the face value, and card processing is included. Partnership rates are negotiated separately and are not on that page. DICE self sign-up terms.
